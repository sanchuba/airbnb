Before your arrival

We live in the house, so check-in is always arranged in communication with you. Clear arrival information helps us welcome you smoothly.

Check-in

Check-in is either in person or via self check-in, depending on time and availability. After 23:00, check-in is always self check-in.

Please message us your expected arrival time as early as possible on the day of arrival.

Guest guide — during your stay

WiFi

Very fast 1 Gbps WiFi, perfect for streaming 4K content, video calls, and using multiple devices at the same time.

Kitchen use

Coffee and tea are free of charge.

  • Pots & pans
  • Plates & cutlery
  • Seasoning & oil
  • You have your own numbered section in the kitchen cabinet
  • You have your own numbered section in the fridge
  • The dishwasher may be used
  • Dirty dishes can be placed inside
  • You do not need to start it
  • You can also wash dishes by hand

Towels & bedding

  • Extra towels and bedding are in the closet
  • Used towels or bedding can be placed in the laundry basket outside your room
  • Need fresh towels? Just send us a message

Washing machine & dryer

  • Located in the basement
  • Free to use
  • Detergent and fabric softener are dispensed automatically, so you do not need to add them manually
  • Please stay home while the dryer is running

Quiet hours

23:00 – 07:00

Please be mindful of other guests who may be resting.

Front door & back door

Front door: it locks automatically when closed. From outside, use the key to open it. From inside, you do not need to lock it.

Back door: please always lock the back door, both when entering and when leaving.

Train track nearby

The house is located next to a train track. Most guests do not experience this as disturbing. If you are a light sleeper, closing the window and air vent at night can help reduce sound.

Supermarket nearby

Albert Heijn is about a 10-minute walk away. Your key includes an Albert Heijn Bonus Card, which you can scan at checkout for discounts on selected products.

Bicycle & locks

A free bicycle is available during your stay.

The bicycle has two locks: a built-in wheel lock and an additional chain lock. Please use both when parking.

Meet Lilly

Our cat Lilly loves attention and pets.

She is not allowed inside your room.

Visitors, check-out & practical notes

Guests & visitors

Visitors are welcome with prior notice.

If a visitor wishes to stay overnight, they must be registered as an additional guest (€20 per night).

Check-out

  • Close all windows
  • Turn off the heater
  • Leave through the front door
  • Drop the keys in the mailbox inside

Cleaning & housekeeping

For stays of 5 nights or longer, we offer complimentary housekeeping in consultation.

  • We always respect your privacy
  • We never enter your room without your permission
  • We will always agree on a suitable time first

Your room has a trash bin.

  • If housekeeping is included, we empty the trash
  • If not, you can dispose of it yourself in the grey bin in the front garden
  • Extra trash bags are inside the bin
  • We keep the full roll inside the bin for convenience
  • When replacing, take a new bag from the roll and leave the roll in the bin

Cleaning supplies available if you prefer to clean yourself:

  • Vacuum cleaner: main floor, near basement door
  • Cleaning supplies: under the kitchen sink

Need help?

For urgent questions during your stay, please send us a WhatsApp message.

Frequently asked questions

Check-in & check-out

Check-in from 16:00. Earlier in consultation. Personal welcome or self check-in. After 23:00 always self check-in.

Check-out before 12:00.

Private apartment?

No. This is a private room in a shared home with the hosts and possibly one other guest.

The kitchen and bathroom are shared.

Is the house quiet?

The house is located near a train track. Most guests do not find this disturbing.

Parking

Parking is free on the street and there is usually space in front of the house.

Bicycle storage

If you travel by bicycle, you can safely store it in our backyard.

For extra security, it is also possible to store your bike inside our lockable shed.

Breakfast

Breakfast is not included. Guests can use the shared kitchen.

Kitchen use

The shared kitchen is available for preparing simple meals.

Washing machine & dryer

Available free of charge in the basement. Detergent and fabric softener are dispensed automatically, so you do not need to add them manually.

Price & invoice

The price includes all costs, including tourist tax.

There are no additional fees, except for an extra overnight guest.

Invoices are available on request. Please send us the email address where the invoice should be sent.

Invoices are sent after check-out.

The rental is exempt from VAT. Therefore, no VAT is shown on the invoice.

Payment

Payment and booking conditions are handled through the platform where you made your reservation. Please check your Airbnb or Booking.com reservation for your payment details and cancellation conditions. Payment on location is not available.

Food delivery

Yes, you can order food and have it delivered to our address.

If your delivery is expected after 23:00, please wait for the courier outside or clearly instruct them not to ring the doorbell, so other guests and residents are not disturbed during quiet hours.

Visitors

Visitors are welcome with prior notice.

Children

The accommodation is suitable for adults only (18+). Children are not allowed.

Long-term stays

We do not offer long-term rentals. Stays are possible for a maximum of 45 days.

Pets

No, pets are not allowed.

Accessibility

The house has one flight of stairs and no elevator.

Air conditioning

There is no air conditioning. A fan is available during warmer months.

Heating

Each room has adjustable heating.

Hairdryer

A hairdryer is available in the bathroom cabinet.

Ironing facilities

An iron and ironing board are available on request.

Contact

Message us through the platform where you made your reservation (Airbnb or Booking.com), or via WhatsApp. For urgent matters, please call. We usually respond quickly.

Parties & events

Parties and events are not allowed.

Need help?

Message us through the platform where you booked or via WhatsApp.
